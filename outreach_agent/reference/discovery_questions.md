# Discovery / Qualifying Questions (Pablo's guide)

Use these when composing a **discovery / qualifying** outreach email (e.g. a lead who
ships with a competitor or 3PL and is open to a comparison). Pick the RELEVANT subset
for the lead — do not dump every question. Keep the email to a few clear questions.

## Parcel / courier discovery
1. How is your shipping going?
2. Are you shipping with anybody else?
3. How much volume are you usually doing **per day**? *(5+ packages/day is good; ~35–50/week)*
   - Are you shipping only within Canada, or to the US too?
   - What is the size of your packages? *(see dimension reference below — internal)*
4. What are you shipping?
5. Is it **B2B or B2C**?
6. Would you mind sharing a **manifest of the last 3 months** of shipments? I don't need
   to see the fees — just the shipments — so we can see how much more value we can
   provide versus who you're shipping with today.

## Freight / pallet discovery (gauge volume / # of skids)
1. I can see you're shipping courier — do you do any **freight**?
2. How many times a week are you shipping **skids**? *(e.g. 3x a week)*
3. Who do you use currently?
4. What **challenges** are you facing currently?

## INTERNAL REFERENCE — do NOT put in customer emails
*(This is for Pablo's own use / call reference only — dimensional-weight math and
surcharge thresholds. Never quote these formulas or thresholds in a client email.)*

- **Dimensional (cubic) weight — Ground:** (L × W × H) ÷ 1728 × 12.4
- **Dimensional (cubic) weight — Express:** (L × W × H) ÷ 1728 × 15
- **Max ideal box size: 18 × 18 × 17.** Over this size → **SHS** (special handling surcharge).
- **Over 50 lb** = overweight → **special handling charges** (heavy/large boxes can't run
  the conveyor and need two people to measure/handle).
